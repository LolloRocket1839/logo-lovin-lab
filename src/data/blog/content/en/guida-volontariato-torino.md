# Complete guide to foundations and volunteering organizations in Turin

Turin offers numerous volunteering opportunities for people of all ages, from 14 years old to seniors. This guide presents the main foundations and organizations active in the area, with information on activities, requirements and contacts.

## Volunteering orientation center

### Vol.To ETS
**Description:** Reference point for those seeking volunteering opportunities in Turin. Offers guidance, training and promotes volunteering in the city.

**Activities:** Volunteering orientation, volunteer training, promotion of volunteering culture, support to associations.

**Minimum age:** 14 years (with parental consent for minors).

**Requirements:** Availability, willingness to help, consistent commitment.

**Website:** [https://www.volontariato.torino.it](https://www.volontariato.torino.it)

## Universal civil service (18-28 years)

### Universal Civil Service
**Description:** National program that allows young people between 18 and 28 years to dedicate one year to community service.

**Activities:** Projects in various sectors: assistance, environment, culture, education, civil protection.

**Age:** 18-28 years.

**Duration:** 12 months.

**Compensation:** Approximately 444.30 euros per month.

**Website:** [https://www.serviziocivile.gov.it](https://www.serviziocivile.gov.it)

## How to become a volunteer: practical steps

1. **Choose the organization:** Identify the cause you're interested in and the most suitable organization.
2. **Contact the organization:** Visit the website or call for information on how to apply.
3. **Participate in an introductory interview:** Many organizations require a meeting to understand your motivations and availability.
4. **Attend a training course:** Almost all organizations offer preparation courses before starting.
5. **Start your journey:** After training, you can begin volunteering activities according to agreed arrangements.

---

## General requirements

- **Motivation:** Passion for serving others and for the chosen cause.
- **Availability:** Regular and consistent commitment over time.
- **Training:** Mandatory participation in training courses.
- **Responsibility:** Seriousness and reliability in assigned activities.

---

## Time required

The time required varies by organization:
- **Minimum commitment:** 2-4 hours per week.
- **Average commitment:** 4-8 hours per week.
- **Intensive commitment:** Universal Civil Service (12 months full-time).

---

## Why volunteer?

- **Help others:** Contribute to community well-being.
- **Personal growth:** Develop skills, empathy and civic sense.
- **New connections:** Meet people with similar interests.
- **Practical experience:** Gain experiences useful for future employment.
- **Make a difference:** Contribute concretely to social change.
