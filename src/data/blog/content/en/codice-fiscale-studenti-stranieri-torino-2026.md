# Codice Fiscale for Foreign Students in Turin: Step-by-Step Guide 2026

> **TL;DR:** The codice fiscale is obtained free of charge in 15 minutes at the Agenzia delle Entrate with your passport and Form AA4/8. Non-EU students staying >90 days also get it automatically at the Questura when applying for a residence permit. Without it, you cannot rent an apartment, open an Italian bank account, buy a local SIM card, or register with the national health system.

---

The codice fiscale is Italy's tax identification code: a 16-character alphanumeric string that uniquely identifies every person in dealings with public administration and private entities. For international students arriving in Turin, it is literally the first document to get — without it, signing a rental contract, opening a bank account, activating a SIM, registering with the NHS, or completing virtually any administrative procedure is impossible.

The good news: it is completely free, requires roughly a 15-minute appointment, and the certificate is issued on the spot.

## The 4 methods to get your codice fiscale {#methods}

### Method 1 — Universitaly portal (non-EU students, pre-enrollment) {#universitaly}

Non-EU students enrolling through the universitaly.it portal can request their codice fiscale directly during the pre-enrollment process. The code is "finalized" automatically once in Italy and registered with the university. This is the most convenient method for those arriving through a formal admissions procedure.

**When to use it:** Non-EU university pre-enrollment via universitaly.it
**Documents:** None additional — it is part of the enrollment process

### Method 2 — Italian consulate abroad (special cases only) {#consulate}

Italian consulates abroad issue the codice fiscale only where it is needed to complete an online procedure that requires the CF before arrival in Italy. Since March 2025 the procedure has become more restrictive — consulates in New York, Boston, Philadelphia, Edinburgh, and San Francisco all issued new guidelines emphasizing that requests are now limited to documented cases of necessity.

**Documents required:** application form, passport copy, written explanation of need.
**Result:** certificate sent by email as PDF.
**When to use it:** only if a specific online procedure requires the CF before your arrival.

### Method 3 — In person at the Agenzia delle Entrate (primary method for everyone) {#agenzia-entrate}

This is the main method and works for everyone — EU and non-EU students, Erasmus, master's students, PhD students. The appointment takes about 15 minutes and the certificate is issued on the spot, on official headed and stamped paper. The plastic tessera sanitaria card with your CF is sent by post in the following weeks.

**How to book:** agenziaentrate.gov.it → "Prenota un appuntamento" → select the circoscrizione (district) of your address/domicile in Turin → choose "Attribuzione codice fiscale".

**Required documents (non-EU students):**

- Valid passport (original + photocopy)
- Study visa (original + photocopy)
- Form AA4/8 (downloadable from agenziaentrate.gov.it or available at the office) — must be signed by hand

**Recommended documents (not mandatory, but helpful):**

- University enrollment/admission letter
- Proof of domicile in Turin (rental contract, accommodation letter, university residence declaration)

**Required documents (EU students):**

- Valid ID card or passport (original + photocopy)
- Signed Form AA4/8

### Method 4 — Questura (non-EU students staying >90 days) {#questura}

Non-EU students staying in Italy for more than 90 days must apply for a permesso di soggiorno (residence permit) within 8 working days of entering Italy, at the Questura di Torino or an authorized post office. During this procedure, the codice fiscale is assigned automatically.

A provisional numeric CF may be issued initially — the definitive alphanumeric CF is assigned when the permit is issued. For immediate use (rental contracts, bank account), the provisional numeric CF is generally accepted.

## Can I do it entirely online? {#online}

No, not via a self-service portal for foreign students. However, it is possible to send the completed Form AA4/8 + passport scan by email to the territorially competent Agenzia delle Entrate office — in this case the certificate is sent back by email within a few days. Online CF "calculators" produce only estimated codes that are not legally valid for any official registration.

## What the codice fiscale is needed for: complete list {#uses}

- ✅ Signing a rental contract
- ✅ Opening an Italian bank account
- ✅ Buying an Italian SIM card
- ✅ Registering with the National Health System (SSN)
- ✅ Accessing EDISU services (scholarships, canteens, halls of residence)
- ✅ Paying university tuition fees
- ✅ Any contract with public or private entities
- ✅ Accessing digital public services (SPID, CIE)
- ✅ Tax returns (where applicable)

## Common mistakes to avoid {#mistakes}

**Using a CF "calculated" online.** These codes are not registered with the Agenzia delle Entrate and are not valid for any official contract.

**Waiting until you've found housing before applying.** Many landlords ask for the CF before accepting a rental offer — apply as soon as you arrive in Turin.

**Booking an appointment at the wrong office.** Offices cover specific districts. Verify which one covers your domicile address before booking.

**Not bringing a passport photocopy.** Many students arrive with only the original — the office does not make copies.

**Confusing the plastic card with the codice fiscale.** The paper certificate issued on the spot is the official document; the tessera sanitaria (plastic card) arrives by post 2–4 weeks later.
