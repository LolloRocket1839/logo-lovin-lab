# Regulated Rent Turin 2026: Complete Guide {#intro}

The regulated rent (canone concordato) is the most powerful tax tool for landlords in Turin. A 10% flat tax, IMU reduced to 4.31‰, and zero registration tax. But the calculation is complex.

This guide explains everything: the 4 zones, sub-bands, step-by-step calculation, compliance certification, and the numbers vs. free-market rent.

---

## Legal framework {#legal-framework}

The canone concordato was established by Law 431/1998 (art. 2, para. 3). Ministerial Decree of January 16, 2017 reformed it: extended the system to all municipalities, introduced mandatory contract templates, and required a conformity certification.

Revenue Agency Circular n. 15/2023 clarified: the certification must be renewed when the territorial agreement or property characteristics change. It doesn't need renewal for a tenant change.

### Turin's Territorial Agreement

The current agreement was filed on December 6, 2022. The update was signed on April 15, 2024, effective May 1, 2024.

Signatories on the landlord side: APE (Confedilizia), UPPI, CONFAPPI, ASPPI, APPC, CONFABITARE, FEDERCASA, FEDERPROPRIETÀ. On the tenant side: SUNIA, SICET, UNIAT, UNIONE INQUILINI, UNIONCASA, ANIAT/CONIA, SAI/CISAL. EDISU participates for student contracts.

## The 4 zones and 3 sub-bands {#zones-bands}

Turin is divided into 4 homogeneous areas (47 OMI microzones):

| Area | Name | Premium zones |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Center | Microzones 1.1p, 2.1p, 3.1p, 4.1p, 5.1p, 6.1p, 7.1p |
| 2 | Semi-center | Microzone 2.2p |
| 3 | Suburbs | None |
| 4 | Hill | Microzones 1.4p, 2.4p |

Premium zones have a single band: €5.00-€8.00/m²/month.

For other zones, the rent depends on property features scoring (22 elements, each worth 0.5 or 1 point):

- **Sub-band 1** (score ≥ 6): highest rents
- **Sub-band 2** (score 4.5-5.5): medium rents
- **Sub-band 3** (score 0-4): base rents

### Rent table (3+2 contract, €/m²/month)

| Sub-band | Center | Semi-center | Suburbs | Hill |
|----------|--------|-------------|---------|------|
| Sub 1 (≥6 pts) | €3.70-€6.50 | €3.70-€5.90 | €3.70-€5.50 | €3.70-€6.50 |
| Sub 2 (4.5-5.5 pts) | €3.10-€5.70 | €3.10-€5.20 | €3.10-€4.70 | €3.10-€5.50 |
| Sub 3 (0-4 pts) | €2.50-€4.60 | €2.50-€4.50 | €2.50-€4.00 | €2.50-€4.30 |

For longer contracts (4+2, 5+2, 6+2) values increase slightly. Example 6+2 Sub 1 Center: €3.90-€6.90. For temporary contracts: same values as 3+2, with +15% if furnished. For student contracts: same values, with +20% if furnished.

## Rent calculation: step-by-step {#calculation}

### Step 1: Calculate usable square meters

Walkable surface at 100%. Garage/box at 80%. Cellar, attics, and balconies at 25%. Exclusive outdoor areas at 10% (max apartment surface). Attics: 100% if height ≥1.60 m, 25% if lower.

### Step 2: Surface coefficient

| Surface | Coefficient |
|---------|------------|
| ≤41 m² | +30% |
| 41.1-51 m² | +25% |
| 51.1-67 m² | +20% |
| 67.1-80 m² | conventional minimum 80.40 m² |
| >80 m² | no coefficient |

### Step 3: Identify zone and sub-band

Use the street directory (Annex 1 of the TA) for the zone. Count the 22 elements for the sub-band.

### Step 4: Base rent

Conventional square meters × €/m² chosen within the min-max range.

### Step 5: Surcharges and reductions

Always calculated on base rent, not cumulatively:

| Surcharge/Reduction | Percentage |
|--------------------|-----------|
| New build ≤8 years | up to +20% |
| Recent renovation ≤10 years | up to +10% |
| Energy class A1-A4 | +6% |
| Energy class A-C | +5% |
| Energy class D-E | 0% |
| Energy class F-G | -5% |
| Furniture purchased ≤5 years (≥€5,000) | up to +5% |
| No security deposit | +2.5% |
| Early termination with 3-month notice | +2.5% |
| Fully furnished | +10% |
| ≥9 feature elements | +10% |

### Step 6: ISTAT adjustment

75% of the annual consumer price index change. Not applicable with flat tax.

## Certification: who does it, how much it costs {#certification}

The conformity certification is **mandatory** to access any tax benefit.

It is issued by at least one of the organizations that signed the TA. For special cases (partial rentals, ≥9 element bonus, recent renovation) bilateral certification is needed.

**Cost:** €50-150 depending on the organization. Online services (e.g., DokiCasa): €139-149. The certification is exempt from stamp duty and registration tax.

**Required documents:** signed contract, cadastral data, energy performance certificate, feature declaration, any surcharge documentation.

## Tax advantages: the numbers {#tax-advantages}

### Flat tax (cedolare secca)

10% on 100% of rent (vs. 21% free market or IRPEF up to 43%).

### IMU Turin

| Scenario | Effective rate |
|----------|---------------|
| Second homes (base) | 10.6‰ |
| Regulated rent with tenant residence | 4.31‰ |
| Regulated rent without residence (temporary) | 7.95‰ |

### Registration tax

Zero with flat tax. Without flat tax, reduced to 1.4% (on 70% base).

### IRPEF (without flat tax)

Taxable base reduced to 66.5% of rent.

### Practical example

On annual rent of €7,200:
- **Free market + IRPEF 38%:** ~€3,804 total (taxes + IMU)
- **Regulated + flat tax 10%:** ~€1,151 total
- **Annual savings: €2,653**

## Temporary vs. student contract {#temporary-student}

| Feature | Temporary | Student |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Duration | 1-18 months, non-renewable | 6-36 months, renewable |
| Requirement | Documented specific reason | University/master's enrollment |
| IMU Turin | 7.95‰ | 4.31‰ |
| Furnished surcharge | +15% | +20% |

The student contract has 7 specific evaluation elements: double bathroom for >100 m², 14-18 m²/student, single room, university proximity ≤3 km, transport ≤2 changes, elevator, internet.

## The 12 most common mistakes {#mistakes}

1. Using the regulated form with free-market rent
2. Not obtaining certification (losing all benefits)
3. Temporary contract without documented motivation
4. Not registering within 30 days
5. Not separating rent from condo fees
6. Using cadastral m² instead of usable m²
7. Including onerous clauses void under art. 13 L. 431/98
8. Not reporting IMU destination via TorinoFacile by April 30
9. Confusing sub-band with premium zone
10. Not updating certification when the territorial agreement changes
11. Applying surcharges cumulatively
12. Forgetting the surface coefficient on small apartments
