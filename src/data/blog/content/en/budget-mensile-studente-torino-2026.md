# Monthly Student Budget Turin 2026: What You'll Really Spend {#intro}

Turin is the only major Italian university city with free public transport, canteen meals at €2.50 for scholarship holders, and rents 32% lower than Milan. The minimum sustainable budget starts at €650 per month.

This guide analyzes every expense item with real 2025-2026 data. Two concrete scenarios: minimum and comfortable budgets.

---

Average rent in Turin has grown 56% since 2021. But it remains 32% lower than Milan and 26% lower than Bologna.

## Food {#food}

### EDISU canteens

Five locations: Principe Amedeo, Olimpia, Castelfidardo, Grugliasco, River Canteen (Murazzi). Full meals at ISEE-based rates:

- Scholarship holders: ~€2.50-3.00
- Full rate: €7-8
- Eligible non-recipients: 1 free meal per day

### Monthly food spending

| Approach | €/month |
|----------|---------|
| Cooking at home (Lidl, Eurospin, Porta Palazzo) | €120-200 |
| Mix canteen + groceries | €200-280 |
| Mainly canteen | €300-320 |

**Tip:** Porta Palazzo market offers unbeatable prices on fruit and vegetables.

## Transport: Piemove makes it free {#transport}

The biggest news for 2025-2026: **Piemove** (Piemonte Viaggia Studia). A completely free pass for students under 26 enrolled at UniTo, PoliTo, and UPO, with ISEE ≤ €85,000.

**What it covers:** buses, trams, GTT urban and suburban metro + SFM trains between city stations.

**Funding:** €37.2 million over 3 years. Requirement: 60 validations/year. Online activation at bip.piemonte.it.

### For those without Piemove eligibility

| Option | Cost |
|--------|------|
| GTT Under 26 pass | ~€26/month or €250-300/year |
| Scooters (Dott, Lime) | ~€0.25/minute + €1 unlock |
| Single GTT ticket | €2.00 (100 minutes) |

## Financial support {#financial-support}

### EDISU scholarships 2025-2026

Up to €8,336.52/year for non-resident students (ISEE ≤ €26,306.25). In 2024/25: 19,142 scholarships awarded, 100% eligible covered. Total allocation: €105.2 million.

### No-Tax Area

| University | ISEE exemption |
|-----------|---------------|
| PoliTo (enrolled from 2025/26) | ≤ €30,000 |
| UniTo | ≤ €26,000 |
| National minimum | ≤ €22,000 (reductions up to €30,000) |

### Under-31 rent bonus

IRPEF deduction up to €991.60/year (or 20% of rent, max €2,000) for the first 4 years. Total income ≤ €15,493.71. Available for regulated rent contracts.

### Bureaucratic costs for non-EU students

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Residence permit | ~€76-80 (PSE €30.46 + shipping €30 + stamp €16) |
| NHS enrollment | €700/year |
| Regional study tax | €140/year |
| Minimum demonstrable financial resources | €6,079-7,001/year |
