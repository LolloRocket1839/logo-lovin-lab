# Health insurance for international students in Italy 2026: complete guide {#intro}

A specialist visit in Turin costs €100–350 out of pocket. An MRI runs €95–620. A hospital stay without coverage can cost thousands. The Italian NHS at €700/year covers everything.

This guide explains every option for non-EU and EU students: voluntary NHS enrollment, the EHIC card, private insurance, and university health services in Turin. With real costs and step-by-step procedures.

---

## The three starting situations {#situations}

Before choosing coverage, understand which category you fall into:

**Non-EU student** (China, India, Brazil, USA, African countries, etc.): no automatic right to the NHS. Can enroll voluntarily paying €700/year, or opt for private insurance. Voluntary enrollment is almost always the best choice for stays longer than 3 months.

**EU/EEA student** (France, Germany, Spain, etc.) or Swiss: entitled to care through the **EHIC (European Health Insurance Card)**, presenting their national health card. For long stays, they can also register voluntarily at the ASL (often for free or at a reduced rate).

**Italian student or Italian resident:** already enrolled in the NHS through their GP at their local ASL.

## Option 1: voluntary NHS — €700/year, the most complete solution {#nhs}

### What it is and how much it costs

Voluntary enrollment in the **National Health Service (SSN)** for non-EU students is regulated by Budget Law 2024 (L. 213/2023, art. 1, paras. 240–241). The cost is **€700 per year**, paid via **Form F24** (tax code **8846**), not splittable (covers the calendar year, not 12 months from the enrollment date).

### What it covers

By enrolling in the NHS you get **exactly the same rights as an Italian citizen**:

- Free, unlimited general practitioner
- Specialist visits with copay (max €36.15 per visit)
- Free emergency room (for urgent codes)
- Hospital admissions
- Diagnostic tests (reduced copay)
- Maternity and childbirth care
- Mental health and psychological support
- Prescription drugs (variable copay by category)

### Cost comparison with private care

| Service | With NHS | Without NHS (private) |
|---------|----------|----------------------|
| GP visit | Free | Not available |
| First specialist visit | €25 copay | €100–350 |
| Abdominal ultrasound | €36.15 copay | €47–140 |
| MRI | ~€36 copay | €95–620 |
| Hospital stay | Free | €500–5,000+ |
| Emergency room | Free (urgent) | €25 (non-urgent, white code) |

€700/year equals just over two private specialist visits. Any student who has even one health need during the year more than recoups the cost.

### How to enroll at the ASL in Turin — step by step {#enrollment}

1. **Pay the F24:** via online banking or a bank counter, cause "SSN studenti stranieri", tax code 8846, amount €700
2. **Gather the documents:**
   - Valid passport
   - Residence permit (or postal kit application receipt)
   - [Codice fiscale (tax ID)](/blog/codice-fiscale-studenti-stranieri-torino-2026)
   - F24 payment receipt
   - Rental contract or hospitality declaration
   - University enrollment certificate
3. **Go to the ASL office for your district:**

| District | Office address | Hours |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| 1 (Centro, Crocetta) | Via Juvarra 19 (Ophthalmic Hospital) | Mon–Fri 8:00–14:00 |
| 8 (San Salvario, Lingotto) | Corso Corsica 55 | Mon–Fri 8:00–14:00 |
| 3 (San Paolo, Cenisia) | Via Monginevro 130 | Mon–Fri 8:00–14:00 |
| 7 (Aurora, Vanchiglia) | Via Cavezzale 6 | Mon–Fri 8:00–14:00 |
| 4 (San Donato, Parella) | Via Pacchiotti 4 | Mon–Fri 8:00–14:00 |

4. **Choose your GP:** from the list provided by the ASL, preferably close to your home or campus. The first appointment can be booked within days.
5. **Receive your health card:** delivered or mailed within 2–3 weeks. In the meantime, the enrollment receipt serves as your document.

**Total time:** 30–60 minutes at the office if you have all documents. Arrive before 9:00 AM to avoid queues, especially in September.

**ISI Center** (Immigrant Health Information) for foreigners without NHS: Mon–Fri 8:30–12:00 and 1:00–3:00 PM, tel. 011.240.3625.

## Option 2: EHIC for EU/EEA students {#ehic}

The **European Health Insurance Card (EHIC/TEAM)** — printed on the back of your national health card — entitles you to "medically necessary" treatment under the same conditions and costs as NHS residents. No additional cost.

**What it covers:**
- Urgent and medically necessary care
- Emergency room
- Chronic disease management already in progress
- Maternity care (necessary care, not planned delivery)
- Urgent dental care

**What it does not cover:**
- Planned/elective treatments (non-urgent interventions, check-ups)
- Medical transport or repatriation
- Care at private facilities (only NHS-affiliated facilities)

**Important for long stays:** the EHIC is designed for temporary stays. An EU student residing stably in Italy for an academic year can — and in many cases should — voluntarily register at the ASL (often for free by presenting Form **S1** from their national social security institution). This gives access to a permanent GP, eliminating the need to go to the ER for non-urgent issues.

## Option 3: private health insurance {#private}

For those arriving in Italy for a short period (less than 3 months) or who don't yet have a residence permit, private insurance is an alternative:

| Feature | NHS (€700/year) | Basic private (~€98–200/year) | Full private (€600+/year) |
|---------|-----------------|------------------------------|--------------------------|
| GP | ✅ Free, unlimited | ❌ | ❌ |
| Specialist visits | ✅ Copay ~€25 | ❌ | ✅ With limits/deductible |
| Emergency room | ✅ Free | ✅ | ✅ |
| Hospital stay | ✅ Free | ❌ | ✅ With caps |
| Repatriation | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Maternity | ✅ | ❌ | Variable |
| Flexibility | Calendar year | Monthly/annual | Monthly/annual |

For stays longer than 3–4 months, the NHS nearly always wins on cost/benefit. Private policies make sense mainly for those in Italy for just a few months or while waiting to complete the NHS procedure.

### After-hours doctor (Continuità Assistenziale)

For non-urgent problems outside GP hours: **call 116117**, active daily from **8:00 PM to 8:00 AM** and on weekends/holidays also 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Free service with health card.

### Essential emergency numbers

- **112** — European single emergency number (police, ambulance, fire)
- **118** — medical emergency
- **116117** — after-hours doctor
- **800.000.500** — CUP appointment booking (free)
- **011/6637637** — Turin Poison Control Center

## Free university health services {#university-services}

Both UniTo and PoliTo offer free psychological and health services for enrolled students:

**UniTo — Listening Space** (Via Verdi 10): up to 5 free individual sessions with psychologists/psychotherapists, in person and online. Book at 388 1469366. Also DSA and disability support (Via Po 31, tel. 011 6704284).

**PoliTo — Listening Space + Passi@PoliTo:** free counseling for all enrolled students, with a specific program for international students (academic and cultural integration). Access through the orientation service.

## ISEE parificato: accessing benefits if your family is abroad {#isee}

For students with families residing outside Italy, the **ISEE parificato** (ISEE-U Parificato) determines access to EDISU scholarships, reduced university fees, and healthcare benefits. It requires certification of family income and assets in the country of origin, translation and legalization (apostille for Hague Convention countries). Free calculation at CAF offices affiliated with EDISU — e.g., **CAF ACLI Turin** (Via Perrone 3bis/A, tel. 011 5712760).

**What it unlocks:**
- Reduced tuition fees
- EDISU scholarships (up to €8,336/year)
- University housing
- Subsidized canteen
