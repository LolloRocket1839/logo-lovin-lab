# Health Insurance for International Students in Italy 2026 {#intro}

A specialist visit in Turin costs €100-350 out of pocket. An MRI runs €95-620. A hospital stay without coverage can cost thousands. The Italian NHS at €700/year covers everything.

This guide explains the options for non-EU and EU students: voluntary NHS enrollment, the EHIC card, private insurance, and university health services in Turin.

---

## Voluntary NHS: €700/year, full coverage {#nhs}

Voluntary enrollment in Italy's National Health Service (SSN) for non-EU students with a residence permit costs €700/year since January 1, 2024 (Budget Law 2024, L. 213/2023, art. 1, paras. 240-241).

The old cost of €149.77 is no longer valid.

**How to pay:** Form F24 (tax code 8846). Covers the calendar year, cannot be split.

### What it includes

The same rights as Italian citizens:
- Free, unlimited general practitioner
- Specialist visits with copay (max €36.15)
- Free emergency room (red, yellow, green codes)
- Free hospital admission
- Maternity care
- Mental health services

### Required documents

- Enrollment form
- Self-declaration form
- F24 receipt
- Passport
- Residence permit (or postal kit receipt)
- Codice fiscale (tax ID)
- Rental contract or hospitality declaration
- Statistical form

## NHS vs. private insurance {#comparison}

| Feature | NHS (€700/year) | INA Assitalia (~€98/year) | Full private (€600+/year) |
|---------|-----------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| General practitioner | Free, unlimited | Not included | Not included |
| Specialist visits | Copay max €36.15 | With limits | With caps |
| Emergency room | Free | Direct access | Post-payment reimbursement |
| Hospital admission | Free | With caps | With caps |
| Maternity | Included | Not included | Variable |
| Medical repatriation | Not included | Included | Included |

For stays longer than a few months, the NHS at €700/year is far more cost-effective than any alternative.

### Costs without coverage in Turin

| Service | With NHS | Without NHS (private) |
|---------|---------|---------------------|
| First specialist visit | €25 copay | €120-350 |
| Ultrasound | €36.15 | €47-140 |
| MRI | ~€36 | €95-620 |

## EHIC card for EU students {#ehic}

EU students can use the European Health Insurance Card (EHIC/TEAM). It's printed on the back of the national Health Card.

**What it covers:** "medically necessary" treatment under the same conditions and costs as NHS residents. Emergency room, urgent care, chronic disease management, maternity.

**What it doesn't cover:** planned/elective treatments, repatriation, private care.

For stays over 3 months, EU students can voluntarily register at the ASL with a simple domicile declaration, or present Form S1.

## EDISU and health copays {#edisu}

EDISU does not directly provide health coverage. However, scholarship recipients can access copay exemptions through ISEE.

## ISEE parificato: how to calculate it {#isee}

The ISEE parificato (ISEE-U Parificato) is the indicator for students with families living abroad. It sums family income + 20% of assets, converted to EUR at the average annual exchange rate.

**Required documents:**
- Family composition certificate
- Income of all household members (year 2023 or 2024)
- Real estate and financial assets as of 12/31
- Translation and legalization (apostille for Hague Convention countries, consular legalization for others)

Free calculation at affiliated CAF offices. Example: CAF ACLI Turin, Via Perrone 3bis/A, tel. 011 5712760.

**What it unlocks:**
- Reduced tuition fees
- EDISU scholarships (up to €8,336/year)
- University housing
- Subsidized canteen
