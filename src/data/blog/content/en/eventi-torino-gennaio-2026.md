# Events in Turin in January 2026: complete guide

January 2026 is shaping up to be an extraordinary month for Turin, with a calendar packed with cultural, musical, theatrical, and sporting events that will bring the city to life during winter. From the iconic Luci d'Artista to major international exhibitions, from sold-out concerts to theatrical performances, and including sporting events and the passage of the Olympic Flame, the Piedmontese capital offers opportunities for all tastes and age groups.

## Luci d'Artista: the last days of magic

The celebrated **Luci d'Artista** event, now in its 28th edition, will continue to illuminate Turin until **January 11, 2026**. This exhibition transforms the city into an open-air museum with light installations by contemporary Italian and international artists distributed throughout the city. This year the event is enriched with four new works, including "Sex and Solitude" by **Tracey Emin** at the Giardini Reali Bassi and "Mummer Love" by the **Soundwalk Collective** with Patti Smith and Philip Glass in the outdoor courtyard of OGR Torino. The lights are on daily from 6:30 PM to 11:45 PM, offering visitors the opportunity to stroll through squares and streets illuminated by unique artistic creations.

## Exhibitions and art expositions

January 2026 sees Turin as the protagonist of a very high-level exhibition scene, with shows ranging from classical art to contemporary photography.

### Orazio Gentileschi: a traveling painter

The **Sale Chiablese of the Royal Museums** host until **May 3, 2026** the exhibition dedicated to **Orazio Gentileschi** (Pisa, 1563 – London, 1639), one of the most important masters of the Italian seventeenth century. Curated by Annamaria Bava and Gelsomina Spione, the exhibition brings together over 40 works from prestigious institutions such as the Louvre in Paris, the Prado in Madrid, the Vatican Pinacoteca and the Uffizi. The exhibition path traces the artistic itinerary of the painter from Rome to Fabriano, from Genoa to Turin, Paris and London, with particular attention to the Annunciation created in 1623 for the Duke of Savoy, considered one of the peaks of his production. The exhibition is open from Thursday to Tuesday from 9:30 AM to 7:30 PM, with special openings during holidays.

### Contemporary photography and modern art

The **CAMERA - Italian Centre for Photography** presents several exhibitions in progress in January. **"Lee Miller. Works 1930-1955"** (until February 1, 2026) offers an unprecedented exhibition of 160 images by the American photographer from the Lee Miller Archives, documenting her work between war, art and surrealism. The Project Room hosts **"Cristian Chironi. Inhabiting the Image"** (until February 1, 2026), dedicated to the artist's relationship with photography and performance.

The **Gallerie d'Italia** in Turin present **"Jeff Wall. Photographs"** (until January 28, 2026), an important retrospective of the Canadian photographer who transforms reality into artistic vision. The exhibition represents a unique opportunity to admire the work of one of the masters of contemporary photography.

### Art exhibitions at GAM and the Venaria Reale Palace

The **Gallery of Modern Art (GAM)** presents three significant exhibitions. **"Nights"** (until March 1, 2026) is a journey through five centuries of nocturnal representations in art, with stars, dreams and full moons as protagonists. **"Elisabetta Di Maggio. Fragile"** and **"Linda Fregni Nagler"** complete GAM's exhibition offerings for early 2026.

The **Venaria Reale Palace** hosts **"Fernand Léger! Yves Klein, Niki de Saint Phalle, Keith Haring"** (until February 1, 2026), an exhibition that highlights the historical and artistic connection between Léger and the generation of French Nouveaux Réalistes artists, with 70 works in total.

### Exhibitions at the Cinema Museum and oriental art

The **National Cinema Museum** at the Mole Antonelliana presents **"Pazza Idea: pop icons of Angelo Frontoni on display"**, a path dedicated to pop culture through the photographer's lens. Also at the Mole, **"Artist Posters"** explores the moment when cinema becomes painting through historical posters.

The **MAO - Museum of Oriental Art** offers **"Chiharu Shiota. The Soul Trembles"** (until June 28, 2026), an exhibition dedicated to the celebrated Japanese artist curated by Mami Kataoka, director of the Mori Art Museum. The installation explores themes of the soul and memory through the artist's characteristic woven nets.

## Theater and performance

Turin's theatrical scene in January 2026 is extremely rich, with proposals ranging from prose to musicals, from opera to dance.

### Teatro Regio: opera and ballet

The **Teatro Regio** opens 2026 with **"La Cenerentola"** by Gioachino Rossini, on stage from **January 20 to 27**. The production, coming from the Maggio Musicale Fiorentino, features three important debuts: maestro **Antonino Fogliani** takes the podium of the Regio Orchestra and Chorus, while the direction is entrusted to **Manu Lalli** and the leading role to **Vasilisa Berzhanskaya**. The young people's preview (under 30) is scheduled for Saturday, January 17 at 8 PM.

The Teatro Regio also offers **"L'elisir d'amore"** by Donizetti, on stage on January 28, 29, 30 and 31. The program also includes the "Regio Concerts" with appointments on January 14 and 30.

### Teatro Colosseo: great variety

The **Teatro Colosseo** presents an extremely varied season. **Antonio Albanese** returns to Turin with his show "Personaggi" from **January 15 to February 1, 2026**. The show brings back on stage the famous characters created by the Lombard actor, including Epifanio, L'Ottimista, il Sommelier, Cetto La Qualunque, Alex Drastico and Perego.

**Diana Del Bufalo** will star on **January 5, 2026** with "Tra sogni e desideri" (Between Dreams and Desires), a show dedicated to the most beautiful Disney soundtracks, accompanied by the narration of Francesco Pannofino and the Philharmonic Orchestra of Benevento. The show represents a musical journey through over a hundred years of fairy tales and melodies that have marked generations.

**Camilla Boniardi and Guglielmo Scilla** will perform on January 20 and 21. Other appointments include "La Locandiera" by Goldoni for the Christmas period and New Year's, and **Luca Barbarossa** on January 10.

### Musicals and major productions

The Teatro Alfieri hosts **"Grease"** from January 30 to February 2, while other musicals on the program include **"We Will Rock You"** (to the music of Queen) on January 16 at the Teatro Colosseo, and "Cuore di ghiaccio – il musical" on January 26 at the Teatro Cardinal Massaia.

**"Forbici Follia"** (Scissors Madness), the famous interactive comic thriller, continues its tradition with performances at the **Teatro Erba** on January 10-12 and 17-19, celebrating 25 years of success.

### Other theaters

The **Teatro della Concordia di Venaria Reale** offers the **Grand New Year's Gala 2026** and "The Opera Locos" (lyrical comedy) also for New Year's. The theater will also host **Franco126** on January 23 with the "Futuri Possibili Club Tour".

The **Teatro Gioiello** features **"Swan Lake"** with classical ballet, while the **Teatro Gobetti** presents various prose shows including "The Conscience of Zeno" and "Little Birds".

## Music and concerts

January 2026 offers an extraordinary musical calendar with national and international artists.

### Major concerts at Inalpi Arena

The **Inalpi Arena** hosts some of the month's most anticipated musical events. **Lazza** performed on **January 15** (sold out date) with his "Locura Tour 2025", presenting over two hours of music with 37 songs including new tracks from the album "Locura" and his historical hits.

### Concerts in theaters

**Dardust** brings his "Urban Impressionism Tour" to **Teatro Gioiello** on **January 12, 2026**. The concert represents a sonic journey that blends piano and electronics in a unique multisensory experience, with tracks from the latest album and the EP "Urban Impressionism (Piano Solo)".

**PFM – Premiata Forneria Marconi** will be in concert at the **Teatro Alfieri** on Monday **January 12, 2026** with the "Doppia Traccia Tour". The concert will be divided into two parts: the first with the group's greatest hits such as "Impressioni di settembre", "È festa" and "La carrozza di Hans", the second dedicated to songs by Fabrizio De André such as "Il pescatore" and "Volta la carta". Opening will be singer-songwriter Fabrizio Consoli.

**Franco126** will perform on **January 23, 2026** at the Teatro della Concordia di Venaria Reale (according to some sources) or at Hiroshima Mon Amour (according to others), presenting the new album "Futuri Possibili", a project that crosses different genres from trap to ballads, to traditional Italian singer-songwriter songs.

### Classical music

The **New Year's Concert on January 1, 2026** traditionally marks the beginning of the year with classical music. The **RAI Auditorium** hosts the **RAI Symphony Orchestra** in various appointments on January 9-10, 16-17 and 30-31.

The **Sala 500 of the Lingotto Congress Center** presents **Alexandra Dovgan** on January 28 with the series "I pianisti del Lingotto". The **Teatro Regio** also offers lecture concerts on January 22.

## Sports

January 2026 is an important month for Turin sports, with major football events and international events.

### Football

The **Stadio Olimpico Grande Torino** hosts two home matches for Torino FC: **Torino-Parma** on Sunday, January 5 and **Torino-Cagliari** on Sunday, January 26. The most anticipated derby is **Torino-Juventus** on Sunday, January 12, also at the Olympic Stadium.

The **Juventus Stadium** hosts the big match **Juventus-Milan** on Sunday, January 19.

### Ice skating

The **Palavela On Ice 2025/2026** continues throughout January, offering Turin residents and visitors the magic of ice skating in one of the city's most modern facilities. The **Christmas Village in Piazza Solferino** also maintains its skating rink until Epiphany.

### The Olympic Flame of Milano Cortina 2026

An event of historical significance will cross Piedmont from **January 10 to 16, 2026**: the passage of the **Olympic Flame of the Milano Cortina 2026 Winter Games**. The Flame will enter Piedmont on Saturday, January 10 in Cuneo, then touching the Langhe (La Morra, Bra, Alba) on January 11. **On Monday, January 12**, from the capital the Flame will cross Turin and venture towards Valle d'Aosta passing through Venaria Reale, Rivoli, Settimo Torinese and Chivasso. The route will involve a total of 25 Piedmontese municipalities and 4 UNESCO sites.

## Shopping and commercial events

### Winter sales 2026

The much-anticipated **Winter Sales 2026** will kick off on **January 3** throughout Piedmont, with a maximum duration of eight weeks until **February 28, 2026**. The central streets of Turin, from Via Roma to Via Lagrange, together with shopping centers and outlets in the region, will offer discounts on clothing, accessories and household items. It's important to note that in the thirty days before the start of the sales, all promotional sales are prohibited.

### Markets

The **Antiques Market of Piazza Vittorio** is held every first Sunday of the month (therefore on January 5, 2026) under the arcades of the largest arcaded square in Europe. Here 80 exhibitors offer antiques and modernariato, from antique furniture to ceramics, from bronzes to antique laces.

The **Vintage della Gran Madre** takes place on Sunday, January 19 in Piazza Gran Madre di Dio, starting at 10:00 AM. This market is dedicated to vintage and second-hand clothing.

## Special events and cultural initiatives

### New Year's and beginning of the year

The **New Year's Concert on January 1** at the Teatro Regio traditionally marks the beginning of the year with classical music. Various venues and theaters offer special events for New Year's, including the Grand Gala at the Teatro della Concordia and The Opera Locos at the Teatro Colosseo.

### Holocaust Remembrance Day

On **January 27** the **Holocaust Remembrance Day** is celebrated, with events and initiatives throughout the city to remember the victims of the Holocaust. Turin, with its Stolpersteine and numerous places of memory, offers paths and reflections on this fundamental theme.

### Chinese New Year

The **Chinese New Year 2026**, year of the Wood Snake, falls in January and will be celebrated in restaurants and Chinese communities in Turin. The Wood Snake symbolizes wisdom, tranquility and stability.

### Orthodox New Year

On **January 14** the **Russian-Orthodox New Year** is celebrated, one of the most important Russian holidays. This celebration offers the opportunity to discover traditions and specialties of Russian culture also present in Turin.

## Conclusions

January 2026 presents itself as an exceptional month to experience Turin in all its cultural, artistic and sporting facets. From the last days of Luci d'Artista that transforms the city into a luminous open-air museum, to major international exhibitions hosting masterpieces from the world's most prestigious museums, to sold-out concerts and high-quality theatrical performances, the Piedmontese capital confirms its vocation as a leading cultural hub.

The arrival of the Olympic Flame, winter sales, sporting events and numerous initiatives in theaters and museums make this month an ideal period to visit Turin or rediscover it as residents. With winter temperatures that invite you to take refuge in heated theaters, exhibition halls and historic cafes, January offers the perfect balance between high culture and popular entertainment, between tradition and contemporaneity, confirming Turin as one of Italy's most vibrant and culturally stimulating cities.
