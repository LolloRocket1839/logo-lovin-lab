# February 2026 in Turin: love, chocolate, art and opera

February 2026 packs an extraordinary range of experiences into just 28 days. From surrealist photography to opera masterpieces, artisanal chocolate to traditional battles, high-level basketball to intimate museum visits, the shortest month offers a dense calendar of reasons to explore the Piedmontese capital.

## Historical photography: two major retrospectives

February 2026 marks the final month of two significant photographic exhibitions. The first is **"Lee Miller. Works 1930-1955"** at CAMERA – Italian Center for Photography (Via delle Rosine 18), closing on **February 1st**. This retrospective, curated by **Walter Guadagnini** for CAMERA's tenth anniversary, gathers over **160 photographs from the photographer's official archives**, many published for the first time.

Lee Miller (1907-1977) occupies a central place in twentieth-century photography: from her Paris years as **Man Ray's** assistant, where she experimented with **solarization** techniques, to her fashion photography in 1930s New York. The exhibition documents her role as a **war photographer during World War II**, when her reportages for "Vogue" covered London during the Blitz and extended to the **German concentration camps**.

- **Guided tours**: Sundays at 17:00 (€5 + admission)

## CioccolaTò: Turin becomes the chocolate capital

From **February 13 to 17, 2026**, Turin transforms into the **world chocolate capital** with **CioccolaTò**. The event returns to **Piazza Vittorio Veneto** with an extended schedule, embracing both **Valentine's Day (February 14) and Carnival** (Mardi Gras falls on February 17). The previous edition drew more than **100,000 visitors**.

This year features approximately **60 producers with over 70 stands**. Only artisans using traditional methods gain access.

**Event hours:**

| :-- | :-- |
| Friday and Saturday | 10:00-21:00 |
| Sunday, Monday and Tuesday | 10:00-20:00 |

- **Tastings, masterclasses, workshops** and the historic **Choco Tram**

## The Battle of the Oranges: Ivrea's historic carnival

A few kilometers from Turin, on **February 15, 2026**, one of Italy's most singular events unfolds: the **Battle of the Oranges** at the **Historic Ivrea Carnival**. The Ivrea Carnival has been a **UNESCO World Heritage** since 2023.

The carnival season runs from **February 1 to February 18, 2026** (Ash Wednesday).
