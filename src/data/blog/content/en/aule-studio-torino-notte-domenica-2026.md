# Late-night and Sunday study spaces in Turin: complete guide 2026 {#intro}

A specialist exam is in two days, it's 9:30 PM, and the university library closed an hour ago. Your apartment is not the right place to focus. Where do you go?

In Turin the answer is not obvious. Traditional libraries close between 7 PM and 8 PM. Cafés with good WiFi turn off the lights by 10 PM. The real options for studying late at night or on weekends are few, specific, and most online guides do not distinguish them clearly. Here is the complete map with verified hours.

**There is no 24-hour study space in Turin.** The latest closing time is 2:00 AM during exam sessions (EDISU Verdi 26 and Michelangelo). On Sundays the options shrink dramatically: only EDISU and one small civic library with afternoon hours.

---

## The only spaces open until midnight (or later) {#midnight}

### EDISU Verdi 26 and Michelangelo: the absolute reference {#edisu-main}

These two halls are the go-to for anyone who needs to study late in Turin. Access requires the Athena Card (university ID) and mandatory booking via the **Campus Piemonte ID Studyroom** app (available on iOS, Android, Huawei AppGallery). The system generates a QR code to scan at entry and exit.

**EDISU Sala Studio Verdi 26**
Via Verdi 26. City center, near Palazzo Nuovo and Campus Luigi Einaudi.
Capacity: ~308 seats.

| Period | Hours |
|--------|-------|
| Regular academic year | Mon–Fri 8:30 AM – midnight / Sat–Sun 8:30 AM – 10:00 PM |
| Exam sessions (Jan–Feb, Jun–Jul, Sep) | **Every day 8:30 AM – 2:00 AM** |

**EDISU Sala Studio Michelangelo**
Via Buonarroti 17/bis. Porta Nuova / [San Salvario](/blog/san-salvario-guida-studenti) area.
Capacity: ~364–427 seats.

| Period | Hours |
|--------|-------|
| Regular academic year | Mon–Fri 8:30 AM – midnight / Sat–Sun 8:30 AM – 10:00 PM |
| Exam sessions (Jan–Feb, Jun–Jul, Sep) | **Every day 8:30 AM – 2:00 AM** |

Both halls offer WiFi (university or EDISU credentials in the format name.surname@edisu-piemonte.it), power outlets, and vending machines. These are silent study spaces. Hot meals are not allowed; packaged snacks are tolerated.

**Important:** during exam sessions demand grows exponentially. Booking your seat the evening before via the app is strongly recommended for peak hours (10:00 AM – 1:00 PM and 2:30 – 6:00 PM). Time slots after 9:00 PM are generally less crowded.

### Sala Studio Castelfidardo (PoliTo) {#castelfidardo}

Corso Castelfidardo 30/A. Cittadella Politecnica.
Capacity: ~180 seats.
Hours: Mon–Fri **8:00 AM – 11:30 PM** / Sat **8:00 AM – 8:00 PM**. Closed Sundays.
Access: PoliTo badge (PoliTo students) or ID document for external students.

The Politecnico's hall at Castelfidardo is the third reliable evening option, closing at 11:30 PM on weekdays. No app booking required for PoliTo students.

### Variante Bunker (ARCI cultural space) {#bunker}

Via Paganini 0/200. Vanchiglia / Lungo Po area.
Hours: Mon–Fri **9:00 AM – 11:00 PM**. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
Access: ARCI membership card (~€10/year, available on site).

Forty seats in a silent room plus a group study room. WiFi, power outlets, air conditioning and heating. The internal bar offers a 15% discount to ARCI members. Probably the most underrated alternative in Turin for evening study in a non-university environment.

## Options open until 8:00–9:00 PM {#evening}

### Biblioteca "Norberto Bobbio" — Campus Luigi Einaudi {#bobbio}

Lungo Dora Siena 100/A. Campus Luigi Einaudi.
Capacity: ~850 seats across 10,000 m².
Hours: Mon–Fri **8:30 AM – 7:15 PM** / Sat **8:30 AM – 1:15 PM**. Closed Sundays.
Booking: **Affluences** app (mandatory for main sectors; free access to Cognetti and Solari sectors on the second floor).

The largest library in the UniTo system, designed by Norman Foster. Silent environments, abundant natural light, outlets at every seat. The limitation is the schedule: it closes at 7:15 PM.

### PoliTo Libraries (BCI and BCA) {#polito-libraries}

**Biblioteca Centrale di Ingegneria (BCI):** Corso Duca degli Abruzzi 24.
**Biblioteca Centrale di Architettura "Roberto Gabetti" (BCA):** Viale Mattioli 39 (Valentino).
Hours: Mon–Fri **8:30 AM – 8:00 PM** / BCA also Sat **8:30 AM – 2:00 PM**.
Access: PoliTo badge for loans; free with ID for consultation.

### EDISU Sala Ormea {#ormea}

Via Ormea 11 bis/E. [San Salvario](/blog/san-salvario-guida-studenti).
Capacity: ~134 seats.
Hours: Mon–Fri **9:00 AM – 7:30 PM** (exam sessions: +Sat–Sun same hours).

### EDISU Sala Principe Amedeo {#principe-amedeo}

Via Principe Amedeo 48. Quadrilatero Romano.
Capacity: ~96 seats.
Hours: Mon–Sat **8:30 AM – 7:00 PM**. Closed Sundays.

## Sundays: the real options (few, specific) {#sunday}

Sunday is the most critical day to find study space in Turin. Here is the complete list of what is open:

**EDISU Verdi 26 and Michelangelo:** open **8:30 AM – 10:00 PM** year-round, until 2:00 AM during exam sessions. The only large study halls open on Sundays in Turin.

**Mausoleo della Bela Rosin (Civic Library):**
Strada Castello di Mirafiori 148/7. Southern outskirts.
Sunday hours: **3:00 – 7:00 PM** (Nov–Feb: 2:00 – 5:00 PM).
The only civic library in Turin open on Sundays. Small, quiet, not optimal for those living far from Mirafiori, but it exists.

**Cafés and cultural spaces open on Sundays:** some Campus Diffuso and ARCI spaces operate on Sunday mornings, but with limited seats and no guarantee of quiet. The **Circolo dei Lettori** (Via Bogino 9) opens Sunday afternoons as a reading space.

**Coworking with Sunday access:** Toolbox Coworking (Via Agostino da Montefeltro 2) and Talent Garden Fondazione Agnelli offer Sunday access to monthly members. Sunday day passes are generally not available.

## Cafés for studying in the evening: realistically until 10:00 PM {#cafes}

No Turin café is designed as an evening study space. Turin has a different bar culture from London or Berlin. That said, some options work:

**Orso Laboratorio Caffè** (Via Berthollet 30/g, [San Salvario](/blog/san-salvario-guida-studenti)). Specialty coffee, stable WiFi, power outlets. Open until 8:00 PM on weekdays. No pressure to order continuously. Considered the best café for working/studying in the city by residents.

**OGR Social Table** (Corso Castelfidardo 22, Cittadella Politecnica). A 25-meter communal table with USB and power outlets, WiFi included with the bar. Open during event hours; check the calendar at ogrtorino.it.

**Nora Book & Coffee** (Via delle Orfane 24/D, Centro). Bookshop-café with a quiet atmosphere and seating. Variable hours, roughly until 9:00 PM on weekdays.
