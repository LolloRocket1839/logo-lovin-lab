## 💡 Pro Tips from JungleRent

### **To maximize productivity:**

1. **Change environment every 2-3 hours** - Science shows changing space helps memorization
2. **Alternate silent and social spaces** - Libraries for concentration, Diffused Campus for group work
3. **Use Pomodoro technique** - 25 min study + 5 min break (perfect in bars and cafés)
4. **Always book when possible** - EDISU halls and UniTo libraries fill up quickly
5. **Download offline maps** - Some spaces are hidden, useful to have GPS coordinates

### **Mistakes to avoid:**

❌ Going to library without booking (UniTo and EDISU)  
❌ Studying in noisy bars for difficult exams  
❌ Not downloading booking apps  
❌ Ignoring Diffused Campus (underrated and fantastic spaces!)

## ❓ FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

### **1. Which app should I download to book EDISU halls?**
Campus Piemonte (iOS and Android). It's mandatory for Buonarroti and Verdi Halls.

### **2. Are public libraries free?**
Yes, all public libraries are completely free and open to everyone.

### **3. Can I study in groups at university libraries?**
No, libraries require silence. For group work use Diffused Campus or bars.

### **4. Where can I study for free until late?**
EDISU Buonarroti and Verdi Halls (until 2:00 AM during exam period), Comala (until 0:30).

### **5. What is the University Diffused Campus?**
A Turin-unique project: public spaces transformed into informal study rooms. 11 free locations.

### **6. Where can I study outdoors?**
Valentino Park, Colletta Park, Imbarchino, Comala (350 heated outdoor seats).

### **7. Do I need a university card to access libraries?**
- **EDISU**: No, but booking required for some halls
- **Politecnico**: Yes, only for PoliTo students
- **UniTo**: Yes, with university credentials
- **Public**: No, open to everyone
