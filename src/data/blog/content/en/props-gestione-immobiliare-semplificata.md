## The Chaos of Property Management in Italy

Managing rentals and real estate in Italy is an administrative nightmare. Whether you have a single room for rent or an entire portfolio of shared apartments, the behind-the-scenes work quickly becomes unsustainable.

### Bills and Condo Fees: The Impossible Math

Have you ever tried splitting an electricity bill among 4 roommates when one arrived mid-month? Or calculating how much each person owes for condo fees based on their room's square footage?

It's an exercise in patience that always ends with incomprehensible Excel spreadsheets, calculators, and probable errors.

### Late Rent: The Payment Chase

*"Sorry, I'll wire it tomorrow."*

A phrase every landlord knows too well. Keeping track of who has paid, who is late and by how much, becomes a full-time job. And when you have multiple tenants in multiple apartments, chaos is guaranteed.

### Scattered Documents and Mysterious Cashflow

PDF receipts in emails, paper invoices in the drawer, scanned contracts somewhere in the cloud. When tax time comes or an audit arrives, the treasure hunt begins.

And the cashflow? How much are you actually earning? Between rent, condo fees, extraordinary maintenance and utilities, calculating the real return on your properties requires hours of work every month.

## Props: How It Works

Props was born from a real need: to radically simplify the management of rentals and real estate. It's not complex software designed for large real estate agencies, but an agile and intuitive tool designed for landlords like you.

### Automatic and Intelligent Expense Splitting

Forget Excel spreadsheets. Props automatically calculates how to split expenses among your tenants with **four splitting methods**:

- **Equal division**: Same share for everyone
- **By room coefficient**: Based on square meters or room value
- **By days of occupancy**: Perfect when tenants change mid-month
- **Custom**: You define the percentages

Upload a bill, select the method and Props does the rest. Every tenant knows exactly how much they owe and why.

### "Inbox Zero" Dashboard for Payments

Props' dashboard works like an intelligent to-do list. At a glance you see:

- **Overdue rent**: Who hasn't paid and for how long
- **Payments to match**: Transfers received but not yet linked to invoices
- **Expenses to split**: Bills uploaded but not yet divided
- **Payments to confirm**: Transactions awaiting verification

No more manual bank account checks. Props tells you what needs your attention, in order of priority.

### OCR: Digitize Bills in Seconds

Take a photo of the bill, upload it to Props and the **integrated OCR** automatically extracts:

- Total amount
- Issue date
- Reference period
- Supplier

No more manual data entry. Props reads documents for you.

### Complete Contract Management

Every rental contract is tracked with all details:

- Start and end dates
- Monthly rent and security deposit
- Payment due date
- Version history (renewals, modifications)

Props automatically notifies you when a contract is about to expire.

### Real-Time Cashflow and Reports

Stop asking yourself "how much am I earning?". Props generates:

- **Cashflow projections** for 3, 6 or 12 months
- **Income vs expenses history** month by month
- **Reports per apartment** or aggregated
- **Excel and PDF export** for your accountant

View clear charts showing revenue, expenses and net flow. Finally know exactly how your investments are performing.

### Tenant Self-Service Portal

Your tenants have access to their own portal where they can:

- View current balance and upcoming deadlines
- Check payment history
- Report a completed payment

Fewer "did I pay?" questions, more autonomy for everyone.

### Multi-Property Without Stress

Managing multiple apartments? Props lets you switch from one to another with a click, keeping everything organized and separate. Each property has:

- Its own tenants and contracts
- Its own expenses and income
- Its own reports and statistics
