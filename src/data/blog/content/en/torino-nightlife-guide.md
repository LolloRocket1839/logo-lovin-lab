# Turin's Vibrant Nightlife: A Complete Guide to the Best Venues and Districts

Turin's nightlife scene is a dynamic blend of **historic clubs**, **contemporary cocktail bars**, **live music venues**, and **cultural spaces** that cater to every taste and preference. From the bustling San Salvario district to the riverside charm of Murazzi del Po, the city offers an incredibly diverse selection of venues where locals and visitors alike gather to experience the best of Italian nightlife.

## Premier Nightclubs and Dance Venues

### Hiroshima Mon Amour: A Legendary Cultural Hub

**Hiroshima Mon Amour** (83 Via Carlo Bossoli, Torino) stands as one of Turin's most iconic establishments, operating continuously since 1986. This legendary club has evolved into far more than just a dance venue—it's a **cultural institution** that hosts concerts, theater productions, theme nights, and conferences. Located in the Lingotto district, the converted cinema space maintains exceptional sound quality and welcomes diverse musical genres from electronic and indie to rock and experimental performances. The venue is highly inclusive, dedicating specific evenings to people with disabilities, making it a welcoming space for all. Open Thursday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 AM, the club is accessible via public transport, bicycle, or taxi.

**Website:** https://www.hiroshimamonamour.org

### Milk Torino: The Modern Dance Experience

**Milk Torino** (Via Paolo Sacchi, 65, Turin) represents the contemporary face of Turin's clubbing scene. Built on the foundations of the legendary La Gare discotheque, Milk has reinvented itself as a stylish destination for the young, international crowd. The venue features **urban hits, techno nights**, and electronic music across a spacious dance floor complemented by modern decor and colorful lighting. Operating Wednesday through Saturday from 11:30 PM to 5:00 AM, Milk offers an electrifying atmosphere perfect for those seeking cutting-edge dance experiences.

**Website:** https://www.milktorino.com

### Centralino: Historic Underground Dance Palace

**Centralino Club** (Via delle Rosine 16, 10123, Torino) maintains its status as one of Turin's most enduring nightlife destinations, having survived decades of musical trends. The club is immediately recognizable by its **mirrored underground corridor** that leads to separate rooms offering different musical atmospheres ranging from **techno and hip-hop to pop and reggaetón**. The venue frequently hosts university and club nights on a monthly basis, creating a super relaxed atmosphere filled with young people of all nationalities. Operating Thursday through Sunday from 11:00 PM to 4:30 AM, Centralino attracts massive crowds, particularly during its legendary Friday "Bananamia" night.

**Contact:** Available through Turismo Torino

### Audiodrome Live Club: Premium Sound Experience

**Audiodrome Live Club** (Strada Mongina, 9, 10024, Moncalieri) represents a unique venue located in a former industrial building outside the city center—just a 10-minute drive from downtown Turin. Ranked as the **best club in Italy and 20th in the world** by the International Nightlife Association, this venue features two separate halls with **incredible audio and lighting systems**. The industrial setting eliminates volume restrictions, making it ideal for showcasing electronic music and techno at their finest. The club's programming ranges from techno and house nights to concerts and live music events.

**Contact:** https://zero.eu/audiodrome-live-club

### Azimut: Contemporary Techno and House

**Azimut** (Via Modena, 55, 10153, Turin) has been active since the 1980s and continues to attract an attentive and diverse audience interested in **techno, groove, and house music**. The recently renovated venue features two distinct spaces: Azimut Silver, an industrial-style room with curved stadium seating surrounding the DJ console, and Azimut Gold, a smaller intimate space with luxurious finishes and its own bar. The club caters to millennials and serious music enthusiasts exploring new electronic music trends.

**Contact:** https://xceed.me/venue/azimut-club-torino

## Craft Beer Destinations and Gastropubs

### OroBirra: Craft Beer Excellence

**OroBirra** (Corso Regina Margherita 97, 10124, Turin) stands as a premier destination for craft beer enthusiasts. This elegant brasserie-brewery features **over 120 craft beers** from Italy and around the world, available both on tap and in bottles. The welcoming but informal atmosphere makes it perfect for enjoying seasonal dishes paired with quality brews, including vegetarian and vegan options. Highly rated on TripAdvisor as the #5 bar & pub in Turin, OroBirra operates Tuesday through Sunday from 7:00 PM onward.

**Website:** https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187856-d3693599-Reviews-ORO_BIRRA-Turin_Province_of_Turin_Piedmont.html

### Birrificio Torino: Historic Brewery Experience

**Birrificio Torino** (Via Parma 30) offers visitors an experience like stepping back to an early 20th-century brewery. The venue combines nostalgia with state-of-the-art production facilities on open display, brewing excellent craft beer without preservatives in varieties including ales, red beers, and dark beers.

### Birrificio San Paolo: Artisanal Wood Integration

**Birrificio San Paolo** (Via Airasca 11/D) uniquely conveys its products through wood essences, crafting beers with local malts and hops. The brewery's philosophies integrate the natural beauty of materials throughout the experience.

## Live Music Venues and Cultural Spaces

### Jazz Club Torino: Premier Jazz Stage

**Jazz Club Torino** (Piazzale Valdo Fusi, 10123, Turin) represents one of Italy's most important national jazz platforms. Operating seven days a week, the venue hosts up to 9 live concerts weekly featuring **jazz, swing, blues, world music, and avant-garde performances**. The intimate setting creates a magical connection between musicians and audience while offering quality food and drink options. Hours extend from Tuesday through Sunday, 6:00 AM to 2:00 AM.

**Website:** https://jazzclubtorino.net

### Spazio 211: Alternative and Contemporary Music

**Spazio 211** (Via Francesco Cigna 211, 10155, Turin) has earned recognition as a premier venue for **live music, indie acts, and experimental concerts**. Located about 10 minutes north of the city center, the venue hosts international indie acts, Italian artists, and theme nights featuring pop, rock, and folk-rock. The venue's open-air garden becomes a summer stage during the "Spazio 211 Open Air" season (June-September) offering acoustic concerts, poetry readings, and visual art exhibitions.

**Website:** https://www.turismotorino.org/en/spazio-211-open-air-2025/

### FolkClub: European-Class Folk Venue

**FolkClub** (Via Perrone 3/bis, 10122, Turin) operates as one of Europe's finest clubs for live music, with an international reputation. Founded in 1988, the venue produces a weekly concert season from October to May, featuring folk, jazz, and blues performances in an intimate atmosphere. The club's quality and atmosphere have earned it recognition among Europe's best music venues.

**Website:** https://folkclub.it

## Neighborhood Nightlife Districts

### San Salvario: The Beating Heart of Turin's Movida

**San Salvario** represents the epicenter of Turin's evening social scene, located near Porta Nuova Station. This district features hundreds of young people gathering in numerous terraces and on Largo Saluzzo square, particularly after dark. The area offers an impressive concentration of innovative **cocktail bars and craft breweries** spread across Via Sant'Anselmo and Via Giuseppe Baretti. Unlike many tourist-oriented areas, San Salvario maintains authentic local character with unassuming bars serving creative cocktails and authentic Valdostan specialties.

**Key Streets:** Via Sant'Anselmo, Via Giuseppe Baretti, Largo Saluzzo

### Quadrilatero Romano: Historic Center Elegance

The **Quadrilatero Romano** occupies Turin's oldest area at the heart of the historic center, bordered by Piazza Statuto and Piazza Castello. This district features historic venues such as **Caffè San Carlo** and **Caffè Torino** offering elegant atmospheres perfect for aperitifs, along with live music bars and nightclubs. Popular venues include **FolkClub**, **T·Club Cocktail Bar** (Via Sant'Agostino 23/q), and **KM5**, with most venues closing around 3:00 AM.

### Murazzi del Po: Riverside Cultural Space

The **Murazzi del Po** features repurposed 18th-century arches along the riverbank transformed into an innovative nightlife destination filled with **pop-up bars and spontaneous music performances**. This area comes alive on warm evenings with venues like **The Beach** (Murazzi del Po Gipo Farassino, 22), featuring disco vibes, DJ sets, and tech-house music typically operating until 4:30 AM. The venue attracts a diverse crowd and offers a picturesque riverside backdrop for evening entertainment.

### Aurora and Vanchiglia: Alternative Culture

The **Aurora and Vanchiglia neighborhoods** host alternative bars and cultural spaces offering live events, art exhibitions, and concerts where creativity merges with community. These districts appeal to those seeking unconventional nightlife experiences beyond mainstream clubbing.

**Bunker** (Via Niccolò Paganini 0/200) hosts diverse programming within a converted space that also includes **JIGEENYI** (focusing on African culture with themed nights) and **CRIPTA747** (a contemporary arts gallery).

**Q35 Warehouse** and **Recall** offer distinct clubbing experiences, while **Hiroshima Mon Amour** also frequently hosts concerts, theater productions, and avant-garde performances alongside its clubbing nights.

## Planning Your Night Out in Turin

When exploring Turin's nightlife, locals recommend arriving early to avoid lengthy queues, particularly on weekends. Most establishments offer free or discounted entry before midnight or 1:00 AM, with prices increasing as the night progresses. Public transport is readily available, with tram lines and buses operating until around 12:30 AM, with night buses (Nightbuster) running Friday through Saturday until 4:00 AM.
