# What to do in Turin in May 2026: events, exhibitions, concerts and theatre

May is probably the best month to discover Turin. Days get longer, the arcades fill with life and the cultural calendar explodes. International festivals, world-class exhibitions and concerts turn the city into an open-air stage. Whether you're planning a long weekend, a study stay or a cultural holiday, this guide covers everything you need to know.

## The big events you can't miss

### Turin Jazz Festival 2026

The festival that officially opens Turin's cultural season. Ten days of concerts, jam sessions and meetings with artists from around the world. Much of the programme is free and outdoors. Just walk through the centre and you'll stumble into the music.

- **When:** April 25 – May 2, 2026
- **Where:** squares, theatres and clubs across the city
- **Info:** [torinojazzfestival.it](https://www.torinojazzfestival.it)

The mix of international names and local talent makes it unique in Italy. If you arrive in Turin on the last weekend of April, you're right in the thick of it.

### Salone Internazionale del Libro 2026

Italy's biggest publishing event returns to Lingotto. Hundreds of publishers, author meetings, conferences and a whole area for families and schools. It attracts over 200,000 visitors every year. Book accommodation well in advance.

- **When:** May 14–18, 2026 (indicative dates)
- **Where:** Lingotto Fiere, via Nizza 280
- **Info:** [sfrfranceschini.it](https://www.sfrfranceschini.it)

It alone justifies a trip to Turin. If you're looking for a place to stay during those days, move early: the city fills up fast.

### Onde 2026: concerts at the Egyptian Museum

Live music among the halls of one of the world's most important museums. The series offers free or low-cost concerts in a truly unique atmosphere.

- **When:** dates spread across spring and summer
- **Where:** Egyptian Museum, via Accademia delle Scienze 6
- **Info:** [museoegizio.it](https://museoegizio.it)

Check the museum website for exact May dates. The format is intimate and evocative.

## Exhibitions and museums open in May

Turin in May is a city-wide museum. From the grand spaces of Palazzo Madama to the experimental installations at OGR, there's a path for every type of visitor.

### "MonumenTO, Torino Capitale" at Palazzo Madama

A journey through art, history and Savoy identity in the heart of the city. The ideal exhibition for first-time visitors to Turin. From royal residences to industrial Turin, through the Risorgimento.

- **When:** February 26 – September 7, 2026
- **Where:** Palazzo Madama, Piazza Castello
- **Info:** [palazzomadamatorino.it](https://www.palazzomadamatorino.it)

### Contemporary art and photography

**GAM – Gallery of Modern Art** hosts exhibitions like "Notti" and Linda Fregni Nagler's solo show. The programmes fit perfectly with an early May visit.

At **MAO – Museum of Oriental Art**, an exhibition on Asian cultures remains open until June 28. It's part of Turin's Art Week season.

### OGR Torino: art and technology

**OGR Torino** presents projects at the intersection of art and technology. Multimedia installations like "WE FELT A STAR DYING" and "ELECTRIC DREAMS" run until May 10. They alone are worth the trip for anyone who loves visual experimentation.

**Useful links for exhibitions:**

## Concerts and live music to mark on your calendar

May brings the first major dates of the live season. From 12,000-seat arenas to the most intimate clubs on Turin's independent circuit.

### TWICE — "This Is For World Tour"

The Turin leg of TWICE's world tour. An event that attracts K-Pop fans from across Europe and represents a significant music tourism opportunity for the city.

- **When:** May 20, 2026
- **Where:** Inalpi Arena, corso Sebastopoli 123
- **Tickets:** [ticketone.it](https://www.ticketone.it)

### Rockin'1000 — Allianz Stadium

A thousand musicians playing together in a stadium. It's an emotional experience before it's a musical one. Perfect for anyone looking for the arena event they'll remember forever.

- **When:** May 30, 2026
- **Where:** Allianz Stadium, Corso Gaetano Scirea 50
- **Tickets:** [vivaticket.it](https://www.vivaticket.it)

### In clubs and theatres

For those who prefer a more intimate setting, May offers perfect evenings. **Mario Biondi** brings his soul to Teatro Colosseo on May 11. **Sarah Toscano** stops by Hiroshima Mon Amour on the 14th with her "Met Gala Tour 2026".

For the complete May concert schedule, the [Concerts in Turin 2026](https://www.guidatorino.com/concerti-torino/) page is the most up-to-date reference.

## Theatre, musicals and live performances

Teatro Alfieri packs May with a range of shows for different audiences. From family musicals to sharp comedy.

**"Aladin – The Musical"** (May 15–17) is the perfect choice for families with children. Spectacular sets and live songs in a well-tested format.

Those looking for something more sophisticated can try **"Lupin – The Musical"** (May 29–31) or the comic one-man show **"Caveman"** by Maurizio Colombi (May 24).

Mid-month, **Francesco Cicchella** brings "Tante belle cose" to the stage (May 25–27).

All shows and tickets at [teatroalfieri.it](https://teatroalfieri.it).

### The independent scene

Hiroshima Mon Amour and Spazio 211 remain the two poles of underground Turin. Evenings spanning indie, electronics, post-punk and themed showcases. These are the places to go if you want to experience the city like a local.

### Opera at Teatro Regio

Teatro Regio's programme features May titles like "I Puritani" and other opera productions with multiple performance dates. An experience that, in the setting of one of Europe's most beautiful theatres, justifies a trip to Turin on its own.

Programme and tickets at [teatroregio.torino.it](https://teatroregio.torino.it).
