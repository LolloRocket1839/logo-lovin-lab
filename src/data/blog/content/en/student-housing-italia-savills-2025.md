# Student Housing in Italy: Analysis of Savills Research Report 2025

The Italian student housing market is experiencing unprecedented growth. The **Savills Research - Italian Student Housing Spotlight 2025** report provides an in-depth analysis of demand, supply and investment dynamics in the sector. Here are the key data points every investor should know.

## The Italian University Market: Record Numbers

Italy now has **1.96 million university students**, a 3% increase compared to the previous academic year. The national university system comprises 99 institutions, 11 of which are online-only, plus 165 AFAM institutions (Higher Education in Arts, Music and Dance) with approximately 91,000 enrolled students.

Rome leads the ranking in absolute student numbers, with 334,000 enrolled including online universities. Milan holds the record for international students in absolute terms (approximately 18,000), while Bologna achieves the highest share of international students (9.8%) and mobile students (47%).

## The International Student Boom

Italy is becoming an increasingly attractive destination for foreign students. In the 2023/2024 academic year, approximately **96,000 foreign students** were enrolled in Italian universities, a figure **+127% higher than a decade ago**.

This trend is driven by:
- Reputation of Italian universities in international rankings
- Competitive cost of living compared to other European capitals
- Quality programs in design, art, engineering and business
- Cultural appeal and lifestyle

## Student Bed Supply: The Major Gap

The Savills report highlights one of the sector's structural problems: **dedicated accommodation supply covers only 12.6% of demand** from off-campus students.

### Current Stock and Composition

The total stock of student beds in Italy amounts to **83,000 units**:
- **69%** provided by DSU (University Right to Study) - public sector
- **31%** private operators (was 19% in 2021)

The five cities with the largest supply (Milan, Turin, Rome, Bologna, Florence) account for approximately half of the national stock, with Milan hosting 18% of the total.

### Supply-Demand Gap by City

| City | 2027 Pipeline | Estimated Gap | Provision Rate |

| Milan | 8,100 | ~67,000 | 40% |

| Bologna | 1,000 | ~32,000 | 20% |
| Florence | - | - | 26% |
| Rome | - | - | 22% |

These data clearly show the investment opportunity: even after the completion of pipeline projects, the gap will remain significant in all major cities.

Milan remains the most dynamic city with 33% of the total pipeline. Padua emerges as a fast-growing market with 12 new facilities planned, 8 of which are large-scale (>300 beds).

## Record Investments in 2024

The PBSA (Purpose-Built Student Accommodation) sector is the most dynamic segment of the Italian living market.

### Investment Volumes

In **2024**, student housing investments in Italy reached **€270 million** across 12 transactions:
- **80%** of transactions involved conversions of existing assets
- Q1 2025 has already seen €80 million in transactions

Investor interest is focused on:
- **Established markets**: Milan, Rome, Bologna, Turin, Florence
- **Emerging markets**: Padua, Pisa

### Prime Yields Comparison

| Sector | Prime Net Yield Q1 2025 |
|--------|-------------------------|
| **PBSA (Student Housing)** | **7%** |
| Multifamily | 6% |
| Office | 5% |

Student housing offers superior returns compared to other real estate segments, compensating for risk with solid fundamentals (constant demand, long-term contracts).

## Turin Focus: Why It's a Strategic Market

Turin represents one of the most interesting opportunities for investors in the student housing sector:

### Key Data
- **113,000 university students** (3rd largest city in Italy)
- **9% international students** (growing)
- **7 universities**: Politecnico, University of Turin, University of Eastern Piedmont, IED, IAAD, SAA, Conservatory
- **Supply-demand gap**: ~45,000 missing beds

### Local Pipeline
- **2,600 beds** under development by 2027
- The Social Hub (525 beds, opening 2026)
- Camplus and other active operators

### Competitive Advantages
1. **Lower acquisition costs** compared to Milan
2. **Higher yields** (7-9% vs 4-5% Milan)
3. **Growing demand** thanks to Politecnico and UniTo
4. **Urban development** in areas like Aurora, San Salvario, Cenisia

## European Trends: Italy as an Emerging Market

In the European context, Italy positions itself among **high-potential emerging markets** alongside Spain and Portugal. Established markets (UK, Germany, France, Ireland, Netherlands) offer fewer growth opportunities but greater stability.

The UK still attracts over 68% of European capital in the sector, but interest in Southern Europe is growing strongly due to:
- Low provision rate
- Solid fundamentals
- Superior yield potential
- Strategic portfolio diversification

## Conclusions for Investors

The Savills report confirms that **Italian student housing represents an investment opportunity with solid fundamentals**:

1. **Structural demand**: 589,000 off-campus students seeking accommodation, with growing demand
2. **Insufficient supply**: only 12.6% of demand covered by dedicated housing
3. **Attractive yields**: 7% net prime, higher than other asset classes
4. **Controlled pipeline**: 25,000 new beds will not fill the gap
5. **Emerging markets**: Turin and Padua offer the best risk/return opportunities
