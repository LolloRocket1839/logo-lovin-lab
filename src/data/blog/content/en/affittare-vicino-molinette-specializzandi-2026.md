# Renting near Molinette: 2026 guide for medical residents

*Updated May 2026. Prices and availability for the Nizza Millefonti / Lingotto / south San Salvario area.*

You just found out the location of your residency program. You start on November 1, the national SSM ranking comes out in July, and now you need to find a place in a city you probably don't know. This is the guide we wish we had read: no theory, only what to do in the next 60 days if your assigned site is the Molinette hospital cluster.

## Why Nizza Millefonti is the right area if you work at Molinette

Turin's university hospital cluster is concentrated in a small patch of neighborhood. From corso Bramante you can reach Molinette, Sant'Anna, Regina Margherita and the Dental School in 15 minutes on foot. The CTO is 12 minutes away, the Mauriziano 8 by bike. San Giovanni Bosco is the only exception, but tram 4 takes you there directly in about 20 minutes.

For a resident doing night shifts, on-call duty and weekend rotations, living within walking distance changes life: no car, no hospital parking subscription, the chance to go home during a 30-minute break.

## Real timing: what to do in July and August

The national SSM ranking is usually published in late July. From that day a six-week race starts. Anyone looking for housing in Turin then has to compete with three groups: clinical-year medical students moving closer to the cluster, freshly enrolled nursing students, and senior residents changing flats.

- **July**: before the ranking, do mapping. Join area waitlists, reach out directly to landlords who manage student housing. Don't sign anything before knowing your assigned site.
- **August**: first two weeks are dead (everyone is on holiday). The last two see the first listings. Once you know your site, book virtual viewings and plan two days in Turin for in-person visits.
- **September**: last window. From mid-month, single furnished rooms in the area under 700 euro/month disappear.
- **October**: you only find what others rejected. Possible, but expensive.

## What to look for in a listing (and what to refuse)

You are a medical resident with a net salary of about 1,300–1,700 euro a month and a 4–5 year contract. You are everyone's preferred tenant. Use this leverage to require:

- **A registered contract** (4+4, 3+2 canone concordato, or university student contract). An unregistered contract means no residency in Turin, no 19% tax deduction on rent, no legal protection.
- **Autonomous heating or centralized heating with individual metering**. Many 1960s–70s buildings in Nizza Millefonti have flat-rate centralized heating: inflated bills.
- **A washing machine inside the flat**, not in a shared basement.
- **A liveable kitchen** if you share with two other residents on opposite shifts.

Refuse without hesitation: a transitory-use contract longer than 18 months (illegitimate for someone with a 4–5 year SSM contract), under-the-table rent, deposits higher than 3 months, agency "key money" beyond the standard commission.

## Real prices in the area, May 2026

Reference rates from the 2026 canone concordato for Nizza Millefonti, single room in a shared flat (3 rooms, two bathrooms, furnished, autonomous heating): **450–650 euro** a month all included, depending on distance from the Molinette entrance and apartment condition.

Whole one-bedroom furnished flat: **700–950 euro** a month, again depending on location and condition. Three-bedroom for three residents splitting: **900–1,200 euro** total.

Single-room prices below 400 euro near Molinette in May 2026 only exist in three cases: interior rooms with no window, very old apartments on the fifth floor without an elevator, or scam listings. Always verify in person before sending even one euro.

## The right contract if you're in residency

For a resident, the serious options are two:

1. **4+4 canone concordato contract** (law 431/98, art. 2 §3). Capped rent set by the City of Turin's territorial agreements, duration that covers your whole residency, 10% flat tax for the landlord (an incentive for them to keep you).
2. **University student contract** (law 431/98, art. 5 §2). Duration 6–36 months, renewable, capped rent, designed for those with provable university enrollment. It works because you are enrolled as a resident at the UniTo Specialization School.

Transitory-use contracts (1–18 months) are wrong for you: they don't cover the duration of residency and don't give stable residency status.

## Practical services in the area

In Nizza Millefonti you have supermarkets within minutes (Esselunga on corso Traiano, Carrefour Express everywhere), late-night pharmacies, the EDISU university canteen in via Galliari if you're a UniTo resident, and a network of bars that open at 6:00 a.m. for people starting shift at 7:00. Parco del Valentino is 15 minutes by bike for a break.

Night transport: tram 4 runs 24 hours with a dedicated lane, buses 35 and 42 have weekend night runs, metro line 1 connects Carducci–Molinette to the city center in minutes.
