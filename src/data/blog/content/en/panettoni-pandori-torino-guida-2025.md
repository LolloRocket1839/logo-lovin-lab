# Best Panettone and Pandoro in Turin 2025

Turin is the undisputed capital of Christmas desserts in Italy, a city where panettone and pandoro represent more than simple seasonal specialties: they are authentic culinary tradition, the result of decades of research and dedication by master pastry chefs. Whether you're looking for the finest artisanal products made with natural leavening and selected ingredients, or the most refined versions from established brands, this guide will lead you through the best addresses in the city, providing all the necessary information: precise locations, phone numbers, prices, and the specialties that characterize each establishment.

## Traditional Artisanal Panettone: Turin's Excellence {#traditional-artisanal-panettone}

### Pasticceria Racca - An Institution Since 1953 {#pasticceria-racca}

**Pasticceria Racca** represents consolidated excellence in the Turin landscape and has been synonymous with quality panettone in the city for seventy years. Founded in 1953, this pastry shop has transformed its initial presence on Via Bava to the current facility inaugurated in 1965 on Via Vigliani, where since 1997 the public sales point has also been located.

Racca's classic panettone is appreciated for its well-alveolated softness and melt-in-your-mouth texture, enriched with raisins and candied orange that release intense aromas when cut. However, the house offers particularly refined proposals: panettone with **marrons glacés**, panettone with **Moscato d'Asti D.O.C.G. glazed with hazelnuts** and seasonal variants reflecting local tradition.

### Enrico Murdocco - Tellia Lab: Certified Quality {#enrico-murdocco}

Master leavening specialist **Enrico Murdocco** is one of the most authoritative names in contemporary Turin pastry, recognized for his use of live mother dough and long natural leavening. His classic panettone is soft, well-alveolated, and pleasantly melt-in-your-mouth, with a butter-rich dough enhanced by raisins and candied orange that release intense aromas immediately when cut.

Murdocco also offers innovative creative versions: the **Raspberry, Coffee and Chocolate** (2025 novelty) combines the pleasant acidity of candied raspberries with the dense flavor of coffee and intense dark chocolate notes. Also available are **Gianduia**, **Apricot and Dark Chocolate**, and **Salted Caramel with Annurca Apple**.

### Purchasing Tips {#purchasing-tips}

**Reservations:** Recommended to book in advance, especially for the most refined creations from renowned producers. Most pastry shops accept reservations until a few days before Christmas.

**Storage:** Once purchased, artisanal panettone and pandoro should be stored in a cool, dry place, away from humidity. Recommended duration is 2-3 weeks, although many keep well for longer periods if properly packaged.

**Online Service:** Many Turin pastry shops offer shipping service. Stratta, Racca, and other major establishments have official e-shops for remote purchases with delivery throughout Italy.

## Conclusion {#conclusion}

Turin represents undisputed excellence in panettone and pandoro production in Italy, a reality where decades-long tradition meets constant pursuit of innovation. From established institutions like Racca and Stratta to contemporary leavening masters like Murdocco and Scovazzo, from the surprising creativity of proposals like Aria's Marino to the classic elegance of Ghigo's Cloud, the choice is vast and always of certified quality.

Whether you prefer rigorous respect for Milanese or Turin tradition, experimentation with unusual ingredients, or simply the pleasure of an artisanal product made with passion and dedication, you will certainly find in the Savoy city the perfect answer to your Christmas gastronomic needs.
