## Complete overview of the January 9-10, 2026 strike

The **union action about to hit the Italian railway system** represents one of the **most significant protests in the transport sector in recent years**. From **9:00 PM on Friday, January 9 until 9:00 PM on Saturday, January 10, 2026**, the national railway system will experience an interruption affecting **Trenitalia, Italo, Trenord in Lombardy, and RFI (Italian Railway Network)**. Additionally, **January 10 will see an additional 8-hour strike by RFI railway infrastructure maintenance personnel**, creating an **unprecedented transport paralysis situation**.

### The reasons behind the protest: a matter of safety and dignity

The unions **CUB Trasporti and SGB** denounce a **structural safety situation that has persisted for years without being adequately addressed**. In 2024, **381 incidents of assault on railway personnel** were recorded, with some particularly serious episodes in December 2024. Assaults take various forms: from verbal insults to spitting, from physical violence to stabbing attempts. FIT CISL stated: "We would be forced, for consistency with the severity of events that occur almost every day, to call a National safety strike every week."

The autonomous unions are demanding:
- **Repopulation of trains, stations, and facilities** with railway personnel
- **Health and safety of operations** for both staff and passengers
- **Single national collective agreement** for all railway companies
- **15-year inflation recovery** on salaries (at least 18%)
- **Reduction of working hours to 36 hours** at same salary
- **Elimination of job blackmail practices**
- **Free union action** and real worker representation

### Procedure to request refund (January 9-10 strike)

**Trenitalia - Intercity and Frecce:**
1. **Timeline:** Request refund from the strike announcement **until the train's departure time**
2. **Online:** Fill out web form at [trenitalia.com/refunds](https://www.trenitalia.com/it/informazioni/la-guida-del-viaggiatore/i-rimborsi.html)
3. **By phone:** Call **800 89 20 21** (free)
4. **At ticket office:** Present documents at any Trenitalia ticket office
5. **Time limit:** Within 12 months from the event date

**Trenitalia - Regional trains:**
1. **Timeline:** Request refund **by 24:00 on the day before the strike**
2. **Same online/phone/ticket office procedure**
3. **Automatic refund:** For regional tickets with automatic refund, **credit within 30 days** if delay >60 minutes

**Italo:**
1. **Timeline:** No specific time limit for strike (automatic refund within 30 days)
2. **Direct refund:** Italo recognizes direct refund without request needed
3. **Phone channel:** 892020 (paid)
4. **Online:** Form on italotreno.it
5. **Credit voucher:** Credit through Italo Credit voucher

**Trenord (Lombardy):**
1. **Timeline:** Request within **30 days of the event**
2. **Number:** +39 02 72 49 31
3. **Online:** [trenord.it](https://www.trenord.it)
4. **Full refund:** If the company is at fault (including strike)
